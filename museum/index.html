Las Meninas is a 1656 painting by Diego Velázquez, the leading artist of the Spanish Golden Age.

It was cleaned in 1984 to remove a yellow film of protective varnish that had darkened over the years.

The painting depicts the Infanta Margaret Theresa, surrounded by her entourage of maids of honor, chaperone, bodyguard, two dwarfs and a dog.
